Who is responsible

My Celestia is a free vision-board and personal goal-planning app provided by Karyna Sapezhko, an individual developer in the Netherlands. For privacy requests, contact support.mycelestia@gmail.com. Karyna Sapezhko is responsible for the processing described here that is carried out for My Celestia.

Your content stays in the app's local storage

Boards, wishes, descriptions, steps, progress notes, amounts, optional profile name, settings, and copies of your selected personal and Pixabay photos are stored on your device. My Celestia has no user accounts or developer-operated cloud synchronisation. Its service does not receive your boards, private texts or personal photos merely because you create, edit or view them. Search words and information you deliberately send to support are separate, as explained below.

Device backups or photo-library synchronisation provided by Apple, Google or your chosen provider may include local files, depending on your device settings. These are not My Celestia cloud backups. Older boards may contain remote Unsplash photos: those images are fetched from Unsplash when displayed and are not guaranteed to remain available offline.

Photos, exports and permissions

The app uses the system photo picker to access photos you choose. It requests photo-library access where the operating system requires it. Saving an exported image to Photos, sharing through another app, and setting a wallpaper on Android happen only at your request. A receiving app handles material you choose to share under its own terms. You can manage permissions in device settings. My Celestia does not request camera, microphone, contacts or device-location access.

Photo search and downloads

When you submit a photo search, the app sends the query, search language, provider and page number to its Railway-hosted service over HTTPS, using POST with a JSON body. Search text is not in the app-to-service URL. The service then sends the query to Pixabay; Pixabay's API uses a query parameter in its HTTPS URL. Avoid entering confidential information into photo search.

The app and service code do not log search text or request bodies. However, the service stores query parameters and results in a shared search cache, without an app account or per-user search history. Results are reused for 24 hours. Entries older than seven days are removed when cleanup runs; removal is not guaranteed at an exact time if the service is inactive.

Photo previews load directly from Pixabay/CDN addresses. When you select a Pixabay photo, the app downloads it through its service and saves a local copy. The service may temporarily cache the selected public image. Pixabay provenance remains available in the app. Railway, image providers and network operators can process IP addresses, request times, requested resources and standard connection information; provider retention is governed by their own practices.

On-device tools and Google ML Kit

Search translation runs on your device, and does not translate your wish content. Language models may be downloaded from Google when needed. Object cutouts use Apple Vision on iOS and Google ML Kit subject segmentation on Android; processing happens on the device, and Android may need to download the relevant module.

Local processing does not mean that the SDK has no network activity. Google ML Kit sends technical diagnostics and usage metrics, such as device/app information, installation identifiers, performance, events, errors and language configuration. Google documents internal Firebase services in its translation component. This does not create a My Celestia account or upload your board to Firebase. ML Kit does not send the input text/images or their processing results to Google for translation or segmentation. See ML Kit privacy information.

Support and this website

If you choose to send an email, we receive your email address, message and attachments. The app prepares safe technical details, including app/build version, operating system, device model, app language and problem context. You review and send the message in your mail app; opening it is not sending it. Do not send passwords or sensitive photos. The support mailbox is hosted by Google.

This legal website uses no site-added cookies, analytics, trackers, adverts, external fonts or submission forms. Its host, GitHub Pages, can process standard web-request information. External websites have their own policies.

Purposes and legal grounds

We process search and support information to provide the functions you request (performance of our terms where applicable). Keeping the service reliable, limiting abuse and handling technical problems are legitimate interests, subject to your rights. We process information to meet legal obligations when required, and obtain consent where applicable law requires it. Device permission is not blanket consent to unrelated processing. Providers may process technical information for their own disclosed purposes.

The app has no adverts, paid features, subscriptions, developer-operated behavioural analytics or advertising/cross-service tracking. The developer does not sell personal data. The SDK diagnostics described above remain distinct from those statements.

Retention, deletion and security

Local content remains until you remove it using available app controls or delete the app and its data. Uninstalling may not remove OS backups, exported pictures, shared copies or provider-held information; manage those separately. The developer cannot remotely access or restore your local boards. Downloaded models may remain managed by the SDK or operating system.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request, follow-up issues and applicable legal obligations. Ask us about deletion. Shared search-cache retention is explained above; it is not tied to an account. We may need limited information to locate records and verify a privacy request. We cannot erase another provider's records ourselves.

Release network connections use HTTPS. Device access controls and app isolation help protect local files, but no system is completely secure. Protect your device and keep independent copies of important materials.

Providers and international processing

Technical data and correspondence may be processed outside your country or the EEA. Where GDPR applies, transfers require a valid legal mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards. Provider policies explain their practices; no exclusively Dutch hosting is promised. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to our processing.

Read the official policies: Pixabay, Railway, Google, Apple, GitHub, and Unsplash for legacy remote photos.

Your rights and children

Where GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability as applicable, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. You may complain to your local supervisory authority or the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Email the privacy contact above; we normally respond within one month, with lawful extensions explained when necessary. We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects through automated profiling.

My Celestia is for teenagers and adults and is not specifically directed at children under 13. Local requirements for parental permission can apply at higher ages. If a child has sent personal information to support or through search, contact us so we can assess and address it.
