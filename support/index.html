Need help?

Write to support.mycelestia@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version and a short description of the problem. Explain what you expected and what happened. A screenshot is helpful if it does not reveal private information.

Do not send passwords, API keys or confidential photographs. Sending email is optional. In the app, “Open email” prepares a message that you can review; you add any screenshot and send it yourself in your mail app.

Where are my boards stored?

Your boards, wishes, saved drafts and copies of selected photos are stored locally. There is no Visteli account or developer-operated cloud recovery. Uninstalling or clearing app data may delete local materials. Keep your own copies of important work. An exported image is not a backup of an editable board. System backups and exported photos are managed separately by your device and chosen providers.

Why does the photo library need internet?

Pixabay search and photo downloads need internet. Availability may temporarily depend on Pixabay, the app's service or your connection. Selected Pixabay photos are saved locally; browsing previews needs a connection. Older remote photos may not be available offline. Initial downloads for on-device models may also need internet.

Why does the app need access to photos?

You choose the photos to add to a board through the system picker. If your operating system requires permission, you control it in device settings. Saving a board image to Photos happens only when you choose to save. Android wallpaper installation also requires your action.

On Android 8 and 9, saving opens the system file picker so you can choose where to save the image; on newer Android versions, it is saved to the device photo library.

Privacy and terms

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. For privacy requests, use the same email address. Visteli is developed by Karyna Sapezhko, an individual in the Netherlands.

Why does an exported image not include everything on my board?

You can place objects outside the recommended outline on the board. Saving or sharing includes only the area of the selected format shown inside the frame in the preview. Objects outside that area remain on your board; they are not deleted. Check the preview before saving a board image or wallpaper, or sharing it.
